Switching to live: what happens

  1. You type LIVE and re-enter the dashboard password.
  2. Open paper trades stay paper and finish on their own. Only new trades use real money.
  3. Stakes start at 25% of paper size and go up only when you raise them here.
  4. Live guardrail caps apply (below). They are tighter than paper's.

Keeping the money separate

  • Every trade, order and decision is tagged paper or live.
  • P&L, the scorecard and charts never mix the two. Each screen has a Paper / Live / Both filter.
  • Live trade markers on charts are drawn with a red outline.
