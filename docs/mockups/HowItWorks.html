How it works right now

Written from the active settings (config v13), so it changes when they do. Last updated 10:00:31.

  1. 1
    Every hour, 30 seconds past

    Check the market

    Gets the latest finished price candles from Kraken for your 6 coins. It never uses a candle that's still forming. A coin is skipped for the hour if its data looks wrong, or if the gap between buy and sell prices is wider than 0.3%.
    Right now: all 6 coins OK.
  2. 2
    Every hour

    Decide what kind of market it is

    Sorts the market into one of four moods, checked in this order:
    • Danger (risk_off): prices are swinging wildly, or Bitcoin fell more than 6% in a day. No new trades.
    • Rising (trend_up): Bitcoin is above its 200-day average, the trend is strong (ADX above 22), and at least 4 of the 6 coins are rising.
    • Falling (downtrend): Bitcoin is below its 200-day average and that average is heading down.
    • Choppy (range): anything else.
    Right now: Rising, for 3 days 4 hours.
  3. 3
    Every 4 hours (00:00, 04:00, 08:00 …)

    Look for trade ideas

    Each kind of market has its own set-ups. Only the ones that suit the current mood and are switched on get checked:
    • Buy the dip (trend_pullback): in a rising market, buy when the price pulls back to its recent average and holds there.
    • Buy the breakout (trend_breakout): buy when the price breaks above its highest point of the last 20 four-hour candles. Currently off.
    • Buy low in a choppy market (range_reversion) and Buy a big bounce (oversold_bounce): only used in choppy or falling markets.

    Coins that don't usually move enough to beat the fees are ignored. The typical 4-hour move must be at least 2.0%.

    New set-ups start on trial: they only record what they would have done, with no money involved, until they have 30 trades and a positive record. Failing set-ups switch themselves off if their last 30 trades go badly (worse than −0.2R on average), and you get a message.

    Right now: 1 set-up on trial (vol_squeeze_break, 12 of 30 trades). Next check at 12:00. Last check found 2 ideas: LINK (buy the dip) and ETH (breakout, but that set-up was off, so it was only logged).
  4. 4
    Only when there's an idea to decide on

    Ask Claude

    Claude gets a short summary: the market mood, the ideas, open trades and how each set-up has been doing. For each idea it can:
    • take it, skip it, or make it smaller
    • give the trade more or less room, within set limits
    • switch a set-up off until the market mood changes

    It can't invent new trades, go past the limits, or loosen a safety net. If Claude doesn't answer, or answers in the wrong format, the bot does nothing that hour.

    Right now: 3 calls today, on your Pro plan. Quiet hours don't call Claude.
  5. 5
    Before every trade

    Safety checks

    • Each trade risks at most 0.5% of the balance (£5.06 now). The trade size is set so that hitting the safety net loses about that much.
    • No more than 3 trades open, and no more than 1.5% at risk across all of them. The coins tend to move together, so they count as one group.
    • If the account is down 3% in a day, no new trades until midnight.
    • If it's down 8% in a week, everything pauses until you restart it.
    Right now: 0.75% at risk across 2 trades; no limits hit.
  6. 6
    When a trade is approved

    Place the trade

    Paper mode: a pretend order at the real Kraken price, with real fees taken off. Live mode: a real order on Kraken. Once it fills, a stop-loss order (the safety net) goes onto Kraken straight away, so it works even if the Pi is off.
    Right now: Paper mode.
  7. 7
    All the time

    Look after open trades

    • Every trade has a safety net (stop) from the moment it opens.
    • Dip and breakout trades: once a trade is in profit, the safety net follows the price up and never moves down. That locks in gains if the price turns.
    • Choppy-market trades: sell when the price gets back to its average, or after 24 hours.
    • You get a Telegram message whenever a trade opens or closes.
    Right now: 2 open: SOL (+2.1R, safety net trailing) and LINK (+0.5R).
  8. 8
    Every Sunday

    Review and suggest changes

    Claude looks at the week's results and suggests changes, such as giving a set-up more room. Nothing changes until you approve it on the Current strategy page. You can roll back to any earlier version.
    Right now: 1 suggestion waiting (v14).
  9. 9
    Always

    Check it's worth running

    Everything is compared with two simple alternatives: just holding Bitcoin and just holding cash. If the bot can't beat holding Bitcoin on return compared with its worst dip, it isn't earning its keep. Every hour's full market snapshot is also saved, so any decision can be replayed and new ideas can be tested on real recorded data.
    Right now: bot +1.2% (worst dip −1.8%) vs holding Bitcoin +4.6% (worst dip −7.9%). Too early to judge.
In one sentence: the code decides what's possible and keeps you safe; Claude decides what's worth doing; you approve any change to the rules.
