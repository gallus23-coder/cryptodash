## Summary

- **Candle moves clear Tier 1's round-trip fee (1.3%) only 54.3% of the time on daily closes, 21.2% on 4h closes** (all 6 coins pooled) — most individual candle-to-candle moves are too small to trade profitably one at a time, even before counting false signals.
- **Genuine ≥10% swings happen roughly 1.5 times/month per coin** (4h zigzag, averaged across all 6) — the opportunity is in catching a handful of real trends per month, not in trading every wiggle.
- **Buy-and-hold over this window ranged from ADA at -40.03% to LINK at -4.61%** (after one Tier 1 round trip) — coin selection and timing both matter a lot; see the per-coin table in §5.
- **BTC's 200-day EMA switch rule returned 14.22% with 12.45% max drawdown across 2 trades** — compare against plain BTC buy-and-hold in §5 to see whether the switching actually earned its keep after fees.
- **The equal-weight 6-coin basket returned -22.23%** with 39.56% max drawdown — diversification's net effect on this exact window, after fees.
- **A simple trend-follower (enter +3% off a swing low, trail 5%, 4h candles) caught real ≥10% swings averaging 6.75% after Tier 1 fees, but also hit 41 false starts** costing -194.69% total — see §3/§6 for whether the false-start cost eats the gains.
- **The same idea on daily candles (+5% entry, 8% trail) averaged 5.52% captured per real swing after Tier 1 fees**, with 15 false starts.
- All 6 coins had 200+ days of history before the window started, so every 200-day EMA figure here is a real, fully-warmed-up signal — no fallback needed.

## 3. Realistic capture — trend-follower simulation

Methodology: using the 5%-zigzag's swing lows as candidate entry points (the finest-grained signal available without hindsight), enter once price rises the stated % above that low, then exit when price closes the stated % below the highest close seen since entry. A trade is a "false start" if price never reached +10% above the originating low before the trail stopped it out; otherwise it's counted as "real" and its % captured is reported. All simulated on candle closes only (no intrabar fills), fees deducted at the stated tier as a flat round-trip %.

## Methodology notes / known simplifications

- All simulations use candle **closes** only — no intrabar fills, so real slippage on stop triggers would be somewhat worse than shown here.
- The trend-follower treats each 5%-zigzag low as an independent hypothetical trade — overlapping positions aren't deduplicated or portfolio-constrained.
- EMA switch-rule equity curves charge one round-trip fee per signal flip and compound daily returns while in position; cash periods earn exactly 0%.
- The equal-weight basket rebases each coin to its own window-start price, averages the six rebased paths, and nets a single round-trip fee per coin against that path — it is not a tradeable rebalancing strategy, just a dispersion-vs-average comparison.
- Fee tiers are used exactly as given (round-trip totals); no live TradeVolume/fee-tier data is stored in this DB to cross-check against.
